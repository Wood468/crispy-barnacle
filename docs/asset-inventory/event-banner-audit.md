# Event banner audit (active and upcoming, 8 Nov 2026 to 8 Jan 2027)

Promotional artwork is the default. A banner is used only when it is the event's own official (pokemongo.com) or Leek Duck page image and passes the Release 1.1 rule in `public/event-art.js` (year, article folder, place; never generic/default images). Everything else shows the labelled themed card. Images were viewed before verification (Into the Wild, Twilight Wishes, GO Wild Area: Global, Night Out, Mall Exploration).

Coverage, dated active and upcoming records: **before 27 of 76 (main), after 39 of 72** (duplicate records joined). Excluding the 13 recurring-format records (Raid Hour, Spotlight Hour, Max Battles, raid rotation), which no source illustrates: **27 of 63 before, 39 of 59 after**.

Sources tried per event: the event's own Leek Duck article og:image, its pokemongo.com news/event page og:image (`public/event-artwork.json`, written by the importer with page URL and retrieval date), and banners already held by other records citing the same page.
