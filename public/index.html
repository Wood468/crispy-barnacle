HUNDOS

Nothing matches those filters.

Ranked by estimated damage per second and bulk. Mega and Primal forms only help while they are active.

A hundo is a Pokémon with perfect hidden stats. It is the most sought-after kind to keep for raids and Master League battles.

What the numbers mean

151515

Every Pokémon has three hidden values called IVs: Attack, Defense and HP. Each runs from 0 to 15. A hundo has all three at 15, which the game shows as 100%.

The in-game appraisal calls it a 4-star Pokémon, with every stat bar full.

Where the odds come from

Wild catch, no boost
1 in 4,096
Weather-boosted wild catch (floor of 4)
1 in 1,728
Raid, egg, research or Rocket reward (floor of 10)
1 in 216
Lucky Pokémon (floor of 12)
1 in 64

Standard game mechanics, not taken from the sources above. The "floor" is the lowest each stat can roll.

Slang you will see

  • Hundo: 15/15/15, 100% IV.
  • Shundo: a shiny hundo.
  • Nundo: 0/0/0, the opposite extreme.
  • 4-star: the appraisal tier a hundo falls in, along with a few near-perfect ones.

When it is worth chasing

  • Raids and gyms: a hundo adds a little extra damage and bulk, so it matters less than the right moves.
  • Master League: high IVs are the goal, so hundos are the standard.
  • Great and Ultra League: a low Attack with high Defense and HP often ranks better than 15/15/15.
  • Collecting: a hundo with a special background or shiny is a trophy.

Mark hundos on the Best Pokémon tab. Raid, egg and research rewards give you the best odds.
